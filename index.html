Peer-Reviewed Publications

2026

D-CPCE: Collision-free DNA Payload Encoding with Uncompromised Logical Capacity | Paper (PDF)

Source: MSST 2026 — 42nd International Conference on Massive Storage Systems and Technology (Paper 18)

Co-authored with Xingyu Wang, Yunfei Gu, Chentao Wu, Jiabin Wang, Fei Wang, Jie Li, Guangtao Xue, Minyi Guo (Shanghai Jiao Tong University) et al.

Proposes D-CPCE, the first DNA encoding architecture to fully eliminate primer-payload collisions — preserving 100% of the logical primer library capacity at mass-storage scale, with a 1.18 bits/NT overall encode rate. Research conducted part-time and remotely in collaboration with the SJTU academician team.
